About COMP3612 Assign 1

Comfortable clothing for creative minds.

Our Story

COMP3612 Assign 1 began with a simple belief: that every programmer deserves clothes that not only look exceptional but feel extraordinary. Founded in 2025, our company emerged from a shared frustration with the compromise between quality and affordability in the coding attire market, as well as a strong desire to get an A grade in COMP3612.

Our Values

Q

Quality First

We source only the finest materials. Every stitch, every button, and every detail is carefully considered to create clothes that last.

S

Sustainability

We are committed to responsible manufacturing, thoughtful material choices, and reducing the environmental impact of our clothing.

I

Innovation

We combine traditional tailoring with new ideas to improve comfort, durability, and style for everyday life.
